# Paterson — Brand & Digital Build

**Role:** Head of Marketing & Communications
**Company:** [Paterson Capital](https://patersoncapital.com) — India's oldest wealth management and stockbroking firm (est. 1892)
**Period:** June 2025 – Present

---

## The situation

Paterson had 130 years of institutional credibility and zero digital presence.

- No social media following
- No website traffic
- No inbound lead pipeline
- No brand system or visual identity
- No content engine

The brief: build everything from scratch.

---

## What I built

### Brand Identity
Complete brand revamp — logo, visual identity, colour system, typography, design language, and a full brandbook. Built end-to-end with the internal team, no external agency.

### Owned Media
Created two media properties from zero:

**Taking Stock** — Weekly podcast on markets, wealth, and investing
- 55 episodes
- 5,000+ subscribers
- 1,500+ watch hours/month

**Take One** — Daily financial news show
- ~2,000 views per episode
- 5 days a week, every week

### Email & Lifecycle
- 5,000+ subscribers
- 18–20% open rate
- Weekly newsletters, market updates, product communications

### Performance Marketing
Built the lead generation engine from zero using Meta Ads and Google Ads:
- 500+ qualified leads/month
- ₹80 CPL in a category where ₹300–800+ is the standard

### AI-Powered Tools
Built three production tools using Claude Code — no dev team, no budget:
