# Paterson Brand & Digital Build

**Role:** Head of Marketing & Communications
**Company:** Paterson Capital — India's oldest wealth management and stockbroking firm (est. 1892)
**Period:** June 2025 – Present

---

## The brief

Paterson had 130 years of institutional credibility and zero digital presence. No social following, no website traffic, no inbound leads. The brief was to build the entire brand and digital ecosystem from scratch.

---

## What I built

### 1. Brand Identity
Led a complete brand revamp from scratch — logo, visual identity, design language, typography, colour system, and a full brandbook. Built with the internal team, no external agency.

### 2. Content Engine
Created two owned media IPs:

**Taking Stock** — Weekly podcast on markets, wealth, and investing
- 55 episodes published
- 5,000+ subscribers
- 1,500+ watch hours/month

**Take One** — Daily financial news show
- ~2,000 views per episode
- 5 days a week

### 3. Email & Lifecycle
- 5,000+ email subscribers
- 18–20% open rate
- Weekly newsletters, market updates, product comms

### 4. Performance Marketing
- Built lead generation from zero
- 500+ qualified leads/month
- ₹80 CPL in a category where ₹300–800+ is the norm for financial services
